ABOUT LEARNLOOP

Knowledge is better shared.

LearnLoop is a student-centric peer-to-peer skill exchange platform built around mutual participation and skill development.

Our idea

Students have different skills and interests, but finding someone to learn from or share knowledge with can be difficult. LearnLoop makes peer skill exchange discoverable, structured, inclusive and measurable.

Our core belief

Every student can be a learner in one area and a teacher in another. Learning isn't a one-way journey—it can loop.
